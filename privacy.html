Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-20

The short version

Meowning doesn't have accounts, doesn't show ads, and doesn't track you. Your cat, your alarms and your collection live on your device. We don't collect any data from the app.

What stays on your device

Your cat's name, alarms, gifts, furniture, fish and daily wake-up and sleep records are stored on your iPhone and are not sent anywhere. Like the rest of your iPhone's data, they are included in your own iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer), which is how your cat comes along when you change phones. We can't see or access this data.

No analytics

Meowning contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code. The only numbers we see are the aggregate figures Apple provides to every developer, such as download and sales totals, and usage and crash statistics from people who chose to share them with app developers in their iPhone settings. None of it identifies you.

Purchases

Meowning Plus is sold through Apple. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details. Whether your subscription is active is checked on your device, directly with Apple. We don't run a server for purchases, and no purchase information is sent to us or to anyone else.

Alarms and notifications

Alarms are scheduled with Apple's system alarm service on your device. Nothing about your alarms is sent to us.

Location and weather

This is off unless you turn it on. If you switch on "Match where you are" (tap the window in her room), Meowning asks iOS for your approximate location, rounds it to about 10 km, and uses it to work out sunrise and sunset on your device and to ask Apple Weather for the local weather. The rounded location is kept on your device with your save. It is sent to Apple Weather only to fetch the forecast and is never sent to us. Turn the switch off and the stored location is erased.

Children

Meowning is suitable for all ages and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the data on your device. Copies inside your own iPhone backups stay there until those backups are replaced or deleted. They are your backups and we can't access them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date above. If a future version reads new kinds of data (for example step counts from Apple Health), we'll explain it here before it ships.
