Support

Something not working, or just want to say hi? Here are answers to common questions.

Why didn't my alarm ring?

Make sure you allowed alarms during setup. You can check in iPhone Settings > Meowning. Meowning uses the system alarm service, so it rings even in Silent mode or a Focus.

Will snoozing upset my cat?

Never. She gets up to some cute mischief and leaves a little mess that takes a couple of taps to tidy. She never gets sick, runs away or holds a grudge.

How do I get more fish?

Wake up on time to get a breakfast gift and 10 fish, then sell gifts you don't want to keep. Meowning Plus doubles fish rewards.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Meowning Plus in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to iPhone Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions > Meowning. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.

Will I lose my cat if I change phones?

Not if you move your data across. Your cat and collection are stored on your iPhone and are part of its backup, so they come along when you set up the new iPhone with Quick Start or restore it from an iCloud or computer backup. A new iPhone set up from scratch, without a transfer or restore, starts with a new cat.
